The following structural and systemic deficiencies were identified during the March 2026 campus physical inventory. ManCom members are requested to upvote critical areas to inform the upcoming FY 2027 Budget Proposal.

Executive Directives

View System Recommendations

Critical Actions Required

  • Water Restoration: Immediate action needed to restore campus-wide distribution, including laboratories and pumping systems.
  • Roofing & Waterproofing: Prioritize RC roof slabs and concrete gutters to stop active moisture ingress.
  • Fire Safety: Rehabilitate fire exit stairs and modernize FDAS for life-safety compliance.
  • Sanitary Deficiencies: Repair non-functional floor drains and piping in Residence Halls.
  • Electrical Grid: Replace non-functional outlets and upgrade wiring for modern AV/AC loads.

Opportunities for Improvement

  • Sustainable Energy: Install a Solar Power System to offset high electricity costs (~40,786 kWh avg) and provide backup power.
  • Utility Resilience: Rehabilitate the rainwater collection network for non-potable uses.
